A BIRTHDAY TALE WRITTEN FOR LADY CHARITY

FILES
  index.html  - all the text (invitation, letter, memories, qualities, poem, toast)
  style.css   - colours (see :root at the top), fonts and layout
  script.js   - the EASY EDIT ZONE at the top: birthday date, music, photos, extra verses,
                Royal Dictionary phrases and jar compliments
  images/     - her photos (see images/README.txt)
  music/      - the song (see music/README.txt)

TO VIEW: open index.html in a browser (needs internet once for the Google Fonts).
TO PUBLISH: upload the whole folder to GitHub Pages, Netlify, etc.

CHANGE THE DATE: BIRTHDAY at the top of script.js (the countdown, hero line and finale follow it),
then also edit "fourteenth of November" in the invitation card in index.html.
ADD A MEMORY CARD: copy one <article class="memory"> block in index.html.
